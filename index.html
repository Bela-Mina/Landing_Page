Leonardo di ser Piero da Vinc

Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519) was an Italian polymath of the High Renaissance who is widely considered one of the greatest painters draughtsman, engineer, scientist, theorist, sculptor, and architect and most diverse geniuses in human history

Leonardo da Vinci's four best and most famous works are

Mona-Lisa (c. 1503 -19) : The Mona Lisa is a half-length portrait painting it has been described as the most visited, the most sung about, [and] the most parodied work of art in the world."

The Last Supper (c. 1495 -98) - It shows the moment Jesus tells his disciples that one of them will betray him. Each disciple shows a unique and realistic human reaction.

Vitruvian Man (c. 1490) - It connects art, math, and human proportions. It shows the Renaissance belief that man is a model of the universe.

Lady with an Ermine (c. 1489–90) - It brilliantly bridges art and science, illustrating human proportion and geometry as described by the ancient Roman architect Vitruvius.
